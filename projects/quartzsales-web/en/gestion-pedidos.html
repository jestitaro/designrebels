Module · Order management

Turn order taking into a simple, controlled process that’s always connected to your ERP.

Order Entry lets you enter and manage orders from the point of sale or from your sales team. It works as a self-service layer connected in real time to your ERP for prices, stock, customer accounts and invoicing.

Key benefits

01

Guided,
error-free entry

Quantities are automatically adjusted to case multiples, and prices and commercial terms are applied for each customer.

02

Exception
control

Orders with price changes or discounts go through a configurable approval flow, with full traceability.

03

Limited stock
management

Quota allocation by product and branch, with automatic backorder calculation when demand exceeds availability.

04

Native
ERP integration

Syncs customers, items, price lists and commercial terms; confirmed orders are transmitted automatically.

05

Visibility and
notifications

Sends automatic email confirmations to the customer, the sales team and approvers at every step of the process.
